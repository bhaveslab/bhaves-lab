Things worth knowing

The details.

Can I purchase these shirts yet?

Not yet. The designs are currently being developed and reviewed for production. Checkout is intentionally disabled until samples, prices, and fulfillment are ready.

Which sizes and fits will be available?

Final garment blanks and sizing haven't been selected. NÝRA will publish actual fit information once samples have been reviewed and approved.

Are the graphics final?

The three directions have been approved for development. Print placement, back artwork, color accuracy, and garment production still require Nyriah's review and a physical sample check.

Will NÝRA offer more than T-shirts?

That's the plan. The label has room for clothing, accessories, jewelry, and later lifestyle products, with each new category developed at its own pace.

IN DEVELOPMENT. This collection is a design preview, not a retail launch. No payment information is collected.
