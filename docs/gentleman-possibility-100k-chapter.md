# The Gentleman of Possibility: The $100,000 Chapter

**Status:** Scripted possibility / fiction. Not a report of real funds received.

**Scene — Out of the Clear Blue Sky**

The Gentleman enjoys a quiet morning. Coffee. Beautiful surroundings.
He has no announcement to make, no campaign to chase. Life is already in motion.

His phone lights up.

> Incoming contribution: $100,000.00  
> Sender: Anonymous benefactor

Bhavé studies the screen for a moment. A smile.

**BHAVÉ:** “Well, look at that. Another possibility.”

He places the phone down and returns to his morning.

**NARRATOR:** “One hundred thousand dollars. No instructions. No destination.
Just a new possibility entering a life already in motion.”

The next chapter asks where he will go, whom he will meet, and what experiences
will unfold. The friends he encounters write their own stories.

**Closing:** Life is already in motion. To be continued.
