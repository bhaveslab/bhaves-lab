## Artwork and sample gate

1. Ask each creative owner to review direction, front/back preference, and brand labels. Do not assume approval from a parent is the child's approval.
2. Choose real garment blank, available colors, fit and fabric with the supplier.
3. Obtain supplier printing method (DTG, DTF, screen print or embroidery as applicable), exact printable dimensions, accepted formats, transparency/bleed and minimum stroke requirements.
4. Convert graphics to the supplier's confirmed specifications, produce front/back placement proofs, check spelling, and use a physical color proof.
5. Confirm quote and approval **before** placing any physical order.
6. Inspect and approve a sample before listing the item.

## Commerce gate

Checkout remains absent from the concept pages. Do **not** reuse Bhavé Lab's booking/ebook payment configuration for unrelated brand sales without an explicit merchant-of-record decision and payment-provider eligibility review. Each brand will need its own policies, shipping/returns, taxes, pricing, payment routing, customer communication, and tested fulfillment.
